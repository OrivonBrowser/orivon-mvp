# Devlog — week of 2026-08-31 (compiled 2026-09-06, rewritten 2026-09-09)

## Team message

**Weekly update:**
- **Orivon did the thing Chrome cannot do, for the first time and for real.** An app running inside Orivon reached out to the internet using a permission I had given it, and got stopped the moment it tried to go somewhere I had not allowed. That is the entire reason the product exists, working start to finish
- **Found and fixed a serious safety hole: taking a permission back did not always stop the app.** If you change your mind, "no" has to mean no, straight away. It does now
- **Decided what an app is allowed to ask you for.** An app has to say exactly what it wants up front, in plain numbers you can read before you agree, and it cannot quietly help itself to more. The most sensitive things it could reach for are now off limits unless it asks for each one by name
- **Decided when you get asked.** Nothing interrupts you when you open an app. The question arrives the first time it actually tries to use something of yours, exactly like your phone asking for the camera the first time you tap it
- **Cleared the decision backlog and corrected the record.** 16 questions waiting on me, 6 that genuinely needed me. Also cut an "Open as app" button our own documents described and nobody ever built, and softened a rule that silently blocked out-of-date sites: now you get told, and you choose
- **Our own safety checks caught real problems before anyone could hit them** including an app that could have quietly filled up your hard drive, and a check meant to prove apps stay inside their permissions that turned out never to have run at all
- **Also landed, nothing you would see yet:** Orivon can be installed on a computer for the first time rather than only run by me; apps from the Nostr world can sign you in through it; and the usage statistics only ever send if you say yes
- **Next: the box people actually see.** The "allow this app to..." popup does not exist yet, so in day-to-day use every app still gets a "no". That screen is what I build next
- Alignment call with JB and defined next direction on Marketing
