## Voice note cues

- **Own node, never gateways** — IPFS and Swarm should come from a node on your own machine: trustless and permissionless, or not at all.
- **Next: sharing what you read** — letting people keep sites and serve them to others is the node's next step; today it only reads.
- **Tunnel inside Orivon, not the system** — the tunnel lives in Orivon itself, so anything an app sends rides it, with no drivers to install.
- **Privacy first, then a second thought** — I first sent the nodes through Nym too; then chose speed for them, privacy for people's own traffic.
- **One uBlock, not a hundred** — tunnelled sites share one profile, so a hundred of them still cost one copy of each extension.
- **Installed means kept, nothing more** — an installed app gets the same switch, popup and rules as any website; installing only stores it.
- **Ports any AI can do** — porting should work with any capable AI, and our tools must say where Orivon ends so nobody patches around it.
