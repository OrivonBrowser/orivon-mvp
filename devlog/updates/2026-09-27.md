# Devlog — week of 2026-09-21

## Team message

**Weekly update:**
- **Finished step 6 of 10: Web3 websites open by name** — vitalik.eth loads in under three seconds, every byte checked on your own computer
- **Four more existing apps now run in Orivon** — a crypto exchange, two chat apps, and a crypto wallet still missing its camera
- **Fixed most of 86 spots where apps worked worse than on a desktop** — FreeTube's 100-channel refresh went from 286 errors to none
- **A trust shield now sits in the address bar**, red to green, and rises when a site's files provably match what it published
- **Websites now ask before showing notifications or opening outside links**, and copying text works on every site
- **Decided how camera, microphone and paste will ask permission**; apps can already keep a secret in your computer's password store
- **Reshaped the roadmap** — existing apps first, Web3 names second; the torrent app and Nostr sign-in are ideas for later
- **Moved ported apps into their own project and sorted Orivon's code into clear folders** — nothing changes on screen

## Voice note cues

- **Stopped short of automating ports** — I built the machinery to run ported apps, but not the part that writes ports, with only one done.
- **Letting go of the torrent flagship** — existing apps became the test of the platform, and Web3 names took the torrent app's place.
- **Two questions, not one** — whether a site's files are genuine and whether you reached the real site are separate questions I had tangled.
- **Who gets to vouch for a site** — higher trust levels come from outside reviewers, and I accepted them even from sources we cannot fully verify.
- **Every privacy fix has a price** — any page could time a request to learn which Web3 sites you opened; I chose one cache per site.
- **Widening the door for apps** — an app qualifies by running on the engine FreeTube runs on, even parts written in languages other than JavaScript.
- **No warnings at the top** — a site at the highest trust level now asks for permissions with no warning text at all.
