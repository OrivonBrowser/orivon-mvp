# Devlog — week of 2026-09-28

## Team message

**Weekly update:**
- **Dropped the "MVP" label**: this is Orivon Browser now, gaining features one at a time as real people need them
- **Orivon now works as an everyday browser**: split tabs, profiles, private windows, bookmarks, downloads, saved passwords, reader view and a full Settings page
- **Chrome extensions run, full uBlock Origin included**: on a standard ad-blocking test, Orivon went from passing 10 checks to 90 of 132
- **Apps can now bring their own helper programs**, sealed inside the app's tab with only the permissions you gave the app
- **The Lounge chat app runs its real server inside Orivon**, loaded from our own IPFS server; FreeTube now plays video too
- **Web3 Score is live**: anyone can publish site ratings, people choose whose to trust; ASGARDEX and The Lounge got the first ones
- **Built Orivon Explore**, a directory of 49 Web3 sites with Orivon-ready apps marked; ipfs:// addresses now open straight from the address bar
- **Faster and steadier**: idle memory down a third, a 10 GB slowdown fixed, and about 120 bugs from one full test sweep fixed

## Voice note cues

- **Not an MVP anymore** — I decided this is Orivon Browser itself, gaining the promised features one at a time as needs appear.
- **Raw machine code stays out** — apps needing desktop-style helpers get them only in a sealed web-safe form, under the permissions people granted.
- **The wallet comes back in** — I moved the wallet from out of scope to in, and explored how it could work; nothing built yet.
- **A browser people can switch to** — tabs, extensions, passwords, settings: what someone leaving Chrome expects, so the Web3 parts have daily users.
- **Ratings that cannot spy** — Web3 Score asks about a whole group of sites at once, so a provider cannot pinpoint yours.
- **My provider blocks IPFS** — my home connection hijacks the IPFS servers; Orivon routes around it, but a first load still takes about 47 seconds.
- **Daily use found what tests missed** — using Orivon myself turned up blank menus, a 10 GB slowdown and 35 bugs our automated tests had missed.
