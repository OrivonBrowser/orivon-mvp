Telemetry

Orivon has no ads and never sees what you browse. A count of how long it is used, and on which Web3 and Web2.5 sites, is how we know it is worth building. No page addresses, no IP address, never sold.

What is sent
  • How long Orivon is open and in use each month, and how much of that time is on Web3, Web2.5 and Web2 sites.
  • The names of the Web3 and Web2.5 sites you spend time on and for how long, in a separate report that is not linked to the rest. Web2 sites are never named.
  • A random ID made from your computer that cannot be turned back into anything about you, the app version, and your region (EU, US or other).
  • Never your history, your searches, a page address or your IP address (the server does not keep it). You can change this any time in Settings.
